Reader: a Caribbean diaspora creator, practitioner or community builder who has useful skills but an uncoordinated network. Promise: build one reciprocal knowledge-sharing bridge, document one useful outcome and establish a small repeatable practice. No earnings, sovereignty-as-legal-status or adoption guarantees. ### Seven-day plan and 30-day continuation — proposed workbook

Day 1: privately map five builders/skills/needs. Day 2: choose one need and ask the person what would help. Day 3: agree a small output and boundaries. Day 4: prepare or teach using accessible tools. Day 5: test with the recipient and revise. Day 6: share the useful lesson with up to three willing contacts, without exposing private details. Day 7: record what worked and agree the next step. At day 30: review one completed output, whether the partner found it useful, what each side contributed, and the next sustainable monthly act of circulation. Free/offline participation is valid.

Reusable chapter pattern: source passage → plain-language teaching → consented founder scene or clearly labelled hypothetical → exercise → Major Move → evidence notes. Closing worksheet fields: skill, community need, willing partner (private), agreed output, credit/permissions, due date, feedback, next check-in. No compulsory wallet/app/purchase.

## 4. Founder-story interview questions

These questions request evidence; they do not establish biography. Record answers privately first. For each selected story capture approximate date/place, what happened, your decision, cost/tradeoff, outcome, exact words worth preserving, and permission to name others. Anonymize third parties unless publication permission is clear. Do not commit raw private recordings or personal contact details to this public repo.

1. **Roots / Chapter 1:** What specific moment made “diaspora” personal for you? If your Jamaican-family/New York experience is the right opening, what scene can you actually remember—who was there, what was said, and what did you understand differently afterward?
2. **Migration / Chapter 1:** When did living away from your roots create both an opportunity and a cost? What should the reader hear so “brain circulation” does not erase that cost?
3. **Ownership / Chapter 2:** Tell us about the first time you realized a platform, employer or intermediary controlled something you thought was yours. What did you change, and what remained dependent on someone else?
4. **Creator shift / Chapter 2:** What did you personally build or teach before you had an audience or budget? What useful output could the reader see today, and what part are you comfortable publishing?
5. **Reciprocity / Chapter 3:** Name one real instance when a skill you learned abroad helped someone connected to home. What did they ask for, what did each of you contribute, and what happened afterward? Did your first assumption turn out wrong?
6. **AI bridge / Chapter 4:** Which actual AI-assisted project connects a diaspora skill to a locally defined need? Separate the demonstration from deployed use; what artifact, date and user feedback can we verify? If none is ready, shall we use a clearly hypothetical example?
7. **Protection / Chapter 5:** Describe a collaboration that broke down around credit, money, permissions or responsibility. What was your own part, and which simple agreement would have helped? What details must remain private?
8. **Culture / Chapters 2–5:** What is one Caribbean practice of sharing knowledge or resources you want readers to recognize? Which island/community and personal experience can you speak for without treating the whole diaspora as identical?
9. **Access / Chapters 3–4:** What have you seen work for someone with limited bandwidth, little money or low confidence? What would you ask them to do with a phone, paper and one trusted person?
10. **Sustainability / Chapter 6:** What is a monthly act of circulation you can actually sustain? What do you decline so service does not become burnout or an unlimited coaching promise?
11. **Reader / whole book:** Who is the first reader you picture—name optional, role, goal, pain point? What should that person be able to do by day seven that they cannot do today?
12. **Voice / close:** How would you explain “sovereign communities of creators” in your own words to a young cousin? Which recovered lines sound like you, and which sound too absolute, artificial or sales-heavy? What is the closing Major Move you will stand behind?
