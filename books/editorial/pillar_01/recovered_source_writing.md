The Brain Drain Myth
For generations, the story told about the Caribbean diaspora was a story of departure — of talent
leaving, of potential being extracted, of the islands being drained of their best minds. We were taught to
see migration as loss.

That story was always wrong. The diaspora was never a drain. It was always a distributed network — a
living web of Caribbean intelligence spread across every major city on earth, carrying culture, skills,
and ambition into every industry that would have them.

The Major AI Mindset reframes this completely: Brain Drain becomes Brain Circulation. The
diaspora is not a wound. It is a superpower.


What Sovereign Communities Look Like
A sovereign community is one that creates more than it consumes. One that owns its infrastructure,
trains its own talent, and circulates wealth internally before exporting it.

 — They build platforms, not just profiles

 — They train each other, not just pay outsiders to train them

 — They own equity in what they build together

 — They reconnect diaspora talent with homeland innovation

 — They create economic bridges between islands and global markets




The AI Opportunity Right Now
Artificial intelligence is creating the greatest economic redistribution opportunity of the 21st century. But
only for those who move early, move smart, and move together.

The Network Effect of the Diaspora
Every Caribbean person in London, Toronto, New York, Bali, or Dubai is a node in a distributed
