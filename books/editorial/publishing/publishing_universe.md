## Team and operating model

One shared publishing team serves four editorial collections. A collection is not a separate autonomous agent, nor does creating a role complete its books.

| Role | Responsibility | Concrete output |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing Director | Route work, protect collection identity, maintain queue | Book job and source/version handoff |
| Source Archivist | Recover existing writing and reconcile conflicts | Inventory, recovered passages, evidence gaps |
| Book Author | Develop Ten Pillars, Major Moves and Money writing | Source-backed outline, chapters and Major Moves |
| Integrity Edition Author | Independently adapt adult, teen and child learning | Edition-specific stories, activities, assessments |
| Evidence Editor | Check facts, rights, voice and claims | Claim ledger and revision decision |
| Production Editor | Prepare covers, PDF/EPUB, metadata and proofs | Reviewed exports and release manifest |

Use existing Creative Workflow Orchestrator for the wider creative queue; Major Voice & Doctrine and Raw Thought Intake for founder material; Brand Asset Steward, Layered Cover Builder, Integrity Publishing Design, Motion Graphics Producer and Marketing Asset Packager for their existing specialist work. Do not duplicate or overwrite those skills. Availability must be checked in each new session.

Codex role configuration is in `.codex/config.toml`; instructions are mirrored in `.agents/skills/`. The same six skills are maintained in the user's Skills workspace. New-session discovery/reload depends on the host; saved skill files do not prove every current session has loaded them. Definitions do not provision an OpenAI Agents service, scheduler, external publishing integration or credentials.
