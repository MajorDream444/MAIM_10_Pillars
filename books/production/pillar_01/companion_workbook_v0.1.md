# Diaspora Empowerment — companion workbook v0.1

By Major Dream Williams · English · Based on manuscript v0.2 · 26 October 2026

Use alongside the ebook or audiobook. These worksheets are new production adaptations of the approved manuscript; they are not a separate approved doctrine or finished designed edition. Keep completed answers private. Initials are enough; do not enter private contact details, account credentials or somebody else's sensitive information. Hypothetical examples are not case studies.

## How to use it

Complete a chapter worksheet, try its Major Move, then return to record what happened. You may work at your own pace. The seven-day plan is flexible. Every blank labelled below is a space for your answer, not missing book content.
